Choosing carpet for a home or office

FACEWALL JOURNAL · MATERIAL ADVICE

What to consider before choosing carpet: style, use, care, and floor preparation.

Start with the space

Living rooms, bedrooms, and offices have different needs. Consider foot traffic, cleaning, and how the space is used.

Choose a texture and color

Compare samples in person to see color, texture, density, and how they look in your room’s light. Screen images may differ from the actual material.

Care and maintenance

Ask how to care for your chosen carpet and consider moisture and dirt exposure before deciding.

Assess the site

Send site photos and approximate dimensions so FACEWALL can advise. Materials and installation requirements will be confirmed before quoting.
