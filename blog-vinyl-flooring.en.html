How does LVT Glue-Down differ from SPC Click-Lock?

FACEWALL JOURNAL · MATERIAL ADVICE

Compare LVT Glue-Down and SPC Click-Lock before planning installation, with particular attention to the existing floor.

Installation is the main difference

LVT Glue-Down uses adhesive specified by the manufacturer. SPC Click-Lock joins at the plank edges. Suitability depends on use and site conditions.

Existing flooring and site preparation

Glue-down finishes depend on a smooth, sound substrate because defects may show through. Click-lock products also have flatness and installation requirements.

Consider thickness in context

FACEWALL offers LVT Glue-Down in 2.0 and 3.0 mm and SPC in several thicknesses. Consider other specifications when judging durability.

Get advice before installation

Photograph the existing floor, provide the approximate area, and choose a catalog color. Our team can advise what needs checking on site.
